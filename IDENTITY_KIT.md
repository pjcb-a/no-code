# Identity kit — JP

## Core message

**Name:** John Paul “JP” Jacob  
**Site name:** JP.DEV  
**Role line:** Frontend developer and UI/UX designer  
**One-line claim:** I turn complex workflows into clear, responsive digital experiences.

The visitor is a recruiter, hiring manager, or collaborator reviewing frontend work. The one action the site should lead toward is **contact JP about a frontend opportunity**. Use “View projects” as the supporting action.

## Visual system

| Element | Decision | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Heading and body font | [Manrope](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Manrope), sans-serif fallback | One family, with weight and size doing the hierarchy work. |
| Main ink | `#201C1C` | Body text and headings. |
| Background | `#FAF9F5` | Main page background. |
| Surface | `#FFFFFF` | Cards and form fields. |
| Accent | `#475642` | Links, buttons, focus states, and small brand details. |

Use headings at roughly 40–64 px on desktop and 32–40 px on mobile; body text at 16–18 px with a line height near 1.6. Keep paragraphs around 60–70 characters per line. Use generous section spacing and simple cards so screenshots carry the visual weight. The ink on background has a contrast ratio of about 16:1; accent on background is about 7.4:1.

**Logo:** the wordmark “JP.DEV” in bold Manrope. Use [the JP monogram](assets/jp-mark.svg) as a favicon or small avatar. Keep clear space around it; do not add effects or gradients.

**Two-line style note:** Manrope, warm off-white `#FAF9F5`, near-black `#201C1C`, and deep green `#475642` create a calm, practical frame. Give project screens generous room and let real work, not decoration, be the most memorable image.

## Writing and image rules

- Write in first person, with concrete verbs and honest scope: “I designed,” “I built,” or “I tested” only where the contribution is documented.
- Lead each project with the problem, the role, the work, and the evidence. Avoid claims of impact without a source.
- Use actual product captures for case studies. [CARES](assets/cares-ui.png) and [SubTrack](assets/subtrack-ui.png) are available as initial captures; gather more screens showing a complete user flow.
- Use a real portrait if one is added later. Skip a generated hero illustration because it would compete with the work and could imply a project image that does not exist.
- Keep buttons readable and provide a visible keyboard focus state. Do not place accent text on low-contrast tinted backgrounds.
